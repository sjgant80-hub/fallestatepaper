## For the end user

Generate, preview, and issue UK property documents from 9 templates. Locked regulatory clauses (◊ marker) carry the statutory wording; editable sections let you customise parties, property details, and commercial terms.

**Lettings documents follow the law in England from 1 May 2026 (Renters' Rights Act 2025).** Every private tenancy is now an assured periodic tenancy with no end date; section 21 "no fault" notices can no longer be served; possession needs a section 8 notice on the prescribed Form 3A, and rent increases a section 13 notice on Form 4A. The tool prepares the grounds, dates and wording for those forms; serve the prescribed form itself, unchanged. Check anything unusual against the government's guidance (gov.uk/guidance/renters-rights-act-an-overview-for-landlords) or take legal advice.

### Key rules

- **EA 1979 s.18**: sole/multi agency wording must be included verbatim
- **Housing Act 1988, as amended by the Renters' Rights Act 2025**: assured periodic tenancies; possession only on section 8 grounds (Form 3A); rent increases only by section 13 (Form 4A), once a year with two months' notice; tenants end a tenancy with two months' notice
- **Housing Act 2004**: deposit protection within 30 days, prescribed info (SI 2007/797); possession is refused if the deposit was not protected
- **Tenant Fees Act 2019**: deposit capped at 5 weeks' rent (6 weeks if annual rent is £50,000 or more)
