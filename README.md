## For the end user

Generate, preview, and issue UK property documents from 10 templates. Locked regulatory clauses (◊ marker) preserve statutory wording from the Housing Act 1988, Housing Act 2004, Estate Agents Act 1979, and Deregulation Act 2015. Editable sections let you customise parties, property details, and commercial terms.

### Key rules

- **EA 1979 s.18**: sole/multi agency wording must be included verbatim
- **Housing Act 1988**: AST, S21, S8, S13 statutory requirements
- **Housing Act 2004**: deposit protection within 30 days, prescribed info (SI 2007/797)
- **Deregulation Act 2015**: S21 pre-requisites (gas, EPC, EICR, How to Rent)
- **Tenant Fees Act 2019**: deposit capped at 5 weeks rent
